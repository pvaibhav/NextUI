# v6.15.0-pvaibhav.1

Based on official NextUI v6.15.0 and subsequent upstream updates.

- Add LUT-based display calibration for TrimUI Brick Pro and Anbernic RG40XXV, RG34XXSP, and RGSP.
- Add Off, On, and Custom colour correction modes, with manual red, green, and blue gains.
- Refresh emulator core builds for TrimUI and Anbernic H700 devices.
- Bundle an updater that follows pvaibhav releases and keeps its self-updates on this fork.

[Installation and update guide](https://nextui.loveretro.games/usage/#getting-started).
